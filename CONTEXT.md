# omarhoumz.com

Omar Houmz's personal site: a blog, a list of projects and an about page.

## Language

**Post**:
A dated article on the blog, identified by its slug and published at `/blog/<slug>/`.
_Avoid_: Article, entry, blog item

**OG card**:
The 1200×630 share image generated for a Post, shown when its link is shared.
_Avoid_: Thumbnail, OG image, social image

**Project**:
Something Omar built, listed on `/projects/` with a status: active, unmaintained or archived.
_Avoid_: Work, portfolio item
